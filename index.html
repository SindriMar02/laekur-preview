Dagur

The hotel is a row of houses in a field, and the field is a farm that still works.

Hótel Lækur stands at Hróarslækur, on a working Icelandic horse farm between the Golden Circle and the south coast. The brook the hotel is named after runs through the home field, past the cottages, and out toward the flats. Hekla and Eyjafjallajökull sit on the horizon.

It is family owned, and it has held its operating licence here since 2017. Nineteen rooms, a hot tub and a sauna that cost guests nothing, and a dining room that looks west. Of the seven scores guests give it on Booking, the highest is the one for staff.

Six kinds of room, and two of them look at the water.

Every rate includes breakfast, 11% VAT and the lodging tax. Every room has its own entrance and its own bathroom, a kettle with coffee and tea, a television and free WiFi, and the hot tub and sauna are free to use.

Dinner is where the light goes.

The dining room faces west across the field, which means the last hour of the day happens at the table. There is an a la carte menu, and breakfast is already in the rate.

There is nothing between the field and the sky.
